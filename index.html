Milagros and Martina are independent designers working together as a creative lab for set design, concept development, creative construction and production.

They develop each project from concept to execution, where creativity, construction and materiality come together into a single narrative. Dinners, launches, windows, installations and private celebrations become temporary spaces built piece by piece — objects found, materials shaped, light and proportion considered — until each space holds its own story.
